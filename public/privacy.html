Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Rose Arc Brow & Lash Studio (“Rose Arc”, “we”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

Rose Arc Brow & Lash Studio, Jeffreys Bay, Eastern Cape. Information officer: Mia Rose. Contact: WhatsApp 076 797 2839 or email rosearc.studio@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, WhatsApp, Instagram handle or email) when you book or ask a question.
  • Appointment details: the treatment you booked, dates and payment records.
  • Information you choose to tell us that is needed to treat you safely, such as allergies, sensitivities or eye conditions, and patch test results.
  • Before and after photos, only with your permission.

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or forms. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits in an anonymous way (for example, which pages are viewed and whether a phone or computer was used). It does not use cookies and does not identify you. When you message us, that message is handled by Instagram or WhatsApp under their own privacy policies.

Why we use it

  • To book, confirm, reschedule and remind you about appointments.
  • To carry out your treatment safely.
  • To keep records we must keep by law, such as invoices.
  • To share photos of our work on social media or this website, only if you have agreed.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact and appointment details only for as long as we need them to look after you as a client, then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer (for example tax records). Photos are removed if you withdraw your permission.

Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent for photos at any time. Contact us using the details above.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.
